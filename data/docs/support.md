# Support

## Hours

Support is available Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM Eastern Time. Support is closed on Canadian and US public holidays.

## Response times

Free plan: community forum only, no guaranteed response.
Pro plan: email support with a first response within 24 business hours.
Team plan: email and chat support with a first response within 4 business hours, and an emergency phone line for outages that is open 24/7.

## Contact

Email support@nimbus.example. Include your account ID and project name so we can find your account.
