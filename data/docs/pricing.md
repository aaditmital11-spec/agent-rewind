# Nimbus Cloud plans and pricing

Nimbus Cloud has three plans: Free, Pro, and Team. Prices are per user per month. Annual billing gets two months free.

## Free plan

The Free plan costs $0. It includes 1 project, 2 GB of storage, and 1,000 API requests per day. Free projects are paused after 14 days without activity.

## Pro plan

The Pro plan costs $25 per user per month, or $250 per user per year with annual billing. It includes unlimited projects, 100 GB of storage, and 600 API requests per minute. Pro adds daily backups kept for 7 days.

## Team plan

The Team plan costs $60 per user per month, or $600 per user per year with annual billing. It includes everything in Pro, plus 1 TB of shared storage, 3,000 API requests per minute, single sign-on (SSO), and backups kept for 30 days. Team requires at least 3 users.

## Extra storage

Extra storage on any paid plan costs $0.10 per GB per month, billed at the end of the month.
