# Refunds and cancellation

## Monthly plans

You can cancel a monthly plan at any time. Your plan stays active until the end of the current billing period. Monthly payments are not refunded.

## Annual plans

Annual plans can be refunded in full within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days, you can cancel, but the remaining months are not refunded. To request a refund, email billing@nimbus.example with your account ID.

## Downgrades

When you downgrade from Team to Pro, the change takes effect at the next billing date. Data above the Pro storage limit becomes read-only until you delete it or buy extra storage.
